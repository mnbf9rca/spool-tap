# CoreS3 SE mounts

`cores3_shelf_mount.stl` is a rough one-piece shelf hook with the CoreS3 SE on the right and a removable Grove NFC reader on the left.
It is 120 mm wide and hangs over the top of a roughly 50 mm thick shelf with a 25 mm deep lip; the lower plate bears against the shelf front.
Four M3 screw paths enter from the shelf side before installation, and the reader's side retainers cover only its bottom 20 mm while leaving its Grove cable exit open.
The thin grid breaks up the 38 mm bridge; three tapered ribs reach closer to the mounting-ring face at its upper edge.
The mount needs no magnets; the white PLA prototype has been printed and mounted on the shelf.

Print the STL as exported, with the broad outer face of the top hook on the build plate.
Use white PLA, 0.20 mm layers, four perimeters, and 20% infill for the first fit print.
Inspect the slicer preview for support needs under the projecting CoreS3 bosses and reader cradle.
Try the empty hook on the shelf before installing the electronics, then check screw length and both cable bends.
Hold the mount while lifting the reader until testing shows the gravity hook stays seated.

The earlier magnetic mount setup was printed, assembled, and tested.

- `cores3_wedge_45.stl` is the 45° rear wedge for the CoreS3 SE, with four M3 screw paths and two pockets for 32 × 3 mm magnets.
- `nfc_dock.stl` is a separate holder for the Grove NFC reader, with an open connector end and two pockets for 8.34 × 8.34 × 2 mm magnets.

All STLs are generated by `prototype_mounts.py`, which requires CadQuery.
Run `python cad/prototype_mounts.py` from the repository root to regenerate them.

Place the wedge magnet-pocket face on the build plate; support the large pocket roofs and upper crossbar.
Place the dock magnet-pocket face on the build plate.

Attach the wedge with four M3 screws before bonding the magnets into their outward-facing pockets.
Check screw length against the CoreS3 inserts; the screw heads seat behind the wedge's mounting ring.
The fit, magnetic holding force, and reader dock are still being tested on the hardware.
